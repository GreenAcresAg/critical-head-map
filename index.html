Method (trend-based)

  1. Screen SGMA wells: ≥12 un-flagged readings since Jun 2015, ≥6 yr span, no gap >2.5 yr, recent data.
  2. Collocate DWR InSAR (median of 5 samples within ~110 m) with each well.
  3. Find quiet windows: centered 12-month displacement ≥ −0.067 ft (InSAR accuracy) sustained ≥1.5 yr. Most recent window with ≥2 readings wins.
  4. Estimate = lowest groundwater elevation observed in that window (ft NAVD88). Levels at that elevation did not cause subsidence, so true critical head is at or below it.
  5. Grade by window length, readings, sensitivity to threshold (0.10 ft), and subsidence seen at higher levels (residual / depth mismatch).
  6. No sustained quiet window → not determinable by trend; BMP §5.3.4 calls for empirical / modeling analysis.

Trend-based estimates are approximate by design (BMP §5.3.1). Re-evaluate as data accrue. Download results CSV
